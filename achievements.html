Crafted Sounds - Achievements

Highlights:

  • Existed for over ten years and released over 60 titles on vinyl, CD, cassette tape, and digital formats.
  • Fostered development in artists and label team members alike through asset preparation+delivery, campaign execution, and artist services.
  • Distributed titles across North America (US+Canada), Europe (UK+EU), and Japan. Here some of the stores that support us.
  • Expanded label offerings to include Distro as a service. Learn more here.
  • Curated by DSPs and retailers such as Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon, Tidal, Bandcamp, Nina, Vinyl Me Please, Juno, HHV, Disk Union, and more!
  • Booked and promoted shows for national acts including Jack Stauber, Crumb, feeble little horse, Dummy, and Peel Dream Magazine.
  • Operated the label on the side of full-time professional and educational commitments.


Timeline

2026:
  • Opened up our UK Webstore in partnership with The state51 Conspiracy in London.
  • Celebrated ten years of existence at The New Colossus Festival in NYC.
  • Gaadge and Ex Pilots toured the west coast!
  • BRNDA toured the EU and played Pies Pala Pop Festival in France!
